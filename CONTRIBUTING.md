# Contributing and reporting an issue

Please describe a reproducible implementation or documentation issue. Include:

- Version and SHA256 of the source you used.
- Exchange-qualified symbol, timeframe, chart type and available history.
- Changed inputs, with credentials and private account details removed.
- The confirmed bar time, expected behavior, observed behavior and minimal steps.
- Whether the observation concerns context, paper acceptance, outcomes, research, alerts or presentation.

A screenshot may help, but it should not replace the bar/input details. Use synthetic examples when market-data redistribution rights are unclear. Please do not attach private source, paid indicators, broker credentials, or a third party's restricted datasets.

Proposed changes should explain the calculation or behavior being changed and a relevant example. Preserve copyright and permission notices. Identify reused code and its source/license. Purely changing appearance or names does not establish originality.

Do not submit performance promises, promotional replies, or unsolicited contact details. An implementation check and a trading-performance study are different claims.
